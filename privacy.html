Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Smart Web Tools provides browser-based utilities. The tool pages are designed to process the text and values you enter locally in your browser. The site does not intentionally send the text you enter into these tools to a tool-processing server.

Hosting and server logs

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser details and request logs as part of operating and securing its service. Refer to GitHub's privacy documentation for details about its processing.

Cookies and analytics

The current version of this website does not intentionally set advertising or analytics cookies. If analytics, advertising or other third-party services are added later, this policy must be updated to explain those services and their data practices.

External links

Pages may link to third-party websites. Their privacy practices are controlled by those providers, not by Smart Web Tools.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the public issue tracker. Do not share personal or sensitive information in public posts.
