About Smart Web Tools

Smart Web Tools is a collection of practical online utilities for writing, SEO workflows and everyday tasks. Our aim is to make common jobs easier with clear interfaces that work in a modern web browser.

What you can do here

Use the word counter and character counter to review writing length, the case converter to format text, the SEO snippet preview to draft search-result messaging, and the keyword density checker to review phrase frequency. You can also calculate percentages, generate random passwords and create a robots.txt starter file.

Our approach

We aim to make tools straightforward, explain their limitations and avoid presenting estimates as guarantees. Tool outputs are intended as practical aids. Check important results against the requirements of the platform or professional process you are using.
